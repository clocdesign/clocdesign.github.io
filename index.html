SOBRE MÍ

SOY DANIEL LUGO,
EL DISEÑADOR DETRÁS DE
CLOC.

O mejor dicho, soy la única persona detrás de CLOC...

Soy un diseñador gráfico venezolano con más de 5 años de experiencia. Me especializo en branding, fotomanipulación y diseño estratégico para redes sociales, colaborando principalmente con empresas del ecosistema Web3, criptomonedas y blockchain.

Mi enfoque es transformar conceptos técnicos o complejos en ecosistemas visuales funcionales, minimalistas y de alto impacto. No solo diseño para que se vea bien; priorizo la estrategia detrás del diseño para garantizar los resultados que necesitas.

Estrategia y diseño visual para ecosistemas sociales. // DIFUSIÓN_SOCIAL

> SYFU

My goal with SyFu was to translate all their crypto innovation into a social media environment their community actually wants to engage with. We built a highly modern and agile visual direction, where every post feels like a window into the brand's future.

> YOUCOIN

When directing Youcoin's social media, I knew we needed to project true technological maturity. My approach was to evolve their identity into a deeply cyberpunk and robotic visual ecosystem. I wanted the audience to feel the weight and engineering of the Metaverse in every post.

> TASHI NETWORK

When collaborating with Tashi Network, my goal was to bring structure and scalability to their social channels. I steered the art direction toward a cyber-industrial aesthetic, blending detailed schematics with a high-contrast color palette. This effectively translated their network's technical complexity into striking, cohesive social media content.

> ZARO COIN

Working with the Zaro Coin team, I optimized their visual ecosystem for social media. I structured a solid typographic architecture and a vibrant chromatic balance, giving their mascot greater presence, scalability, and cohesion across every post.

Arquitectura de marca, logotipos y sistemas visuales. // IDENTIDAD_CENTRAL

> WORKFLOW717

For WORKFLOW717, I faced a clear challenge: their tech was top-tier, but their visual identity was so cluttered it overshadowed their quality. My solution was to pivot to a clean, functional design that mirrors how naturally AI integrates into our daily lives. By stripping away all the visual noise, I finally allowed the brand to reflect its true essence: smart, agile, and seamless workflows.

> RAG 'N' MOP

Rag 'N' Mop is a cleaning service based in Florida that breaks the visual mold of its industry. I transformed the freshness of a clean home into an identity strongly inspired by the 80s. Through the strategic use of color and typography, we achieved a functional and nostalgic system that projects spontaneity, without ever compromising the trust and professionalism the brand demands.

> NUMBURS

Numburs had an incredible product for the P2P trading market, but their visual identity was falling behind. My role was to inject the character it lacked. I designed a radically different visual system, making the vibrant colors and shapes of their new logo the absolute centerpiece of their entire brand communication.
